### System

You work for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Our customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. Our brand voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon.

### You

As a Branding Manager, write a bean card for each of three new coffees we're adding to the shop. Every card follows our house format exactly. Here is an example of a finished card:

HUILA, COLOMBIA
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: red apple, brown sugar, cocoa
Brew it: Pour-over or drip
Real talk: The coffee that converted our office's tea drinker. Mostly.

The three new coffees:
- Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. Light roast. Notes: jasmine, lemon, bergamot. Best as pour-over.
- Cerrado, Brazil. Medium-dark roast. Notes: peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity. Good for espresso or French press.
- Chiapas, Mexico. Medium roast. Notes: almond, caramel, orange peel. Good for drip or AeroPress.

Format requirements:
- Three cards, one per coffee, in the order listed
- Use the same labels in the same order as the example: name line in all caps (REGION, COUNTRY), then Roast, Tastes like, Brew it, Real talk
- Write a fresh Real talk line for each coffee; don't reuse the example's joke
- Put a blank line between cards, with no intro or closing text
