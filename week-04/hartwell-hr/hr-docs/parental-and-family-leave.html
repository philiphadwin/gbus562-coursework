Parental and Family Leave Policy

Version 3.0, current. Owner: Human Resources.

Who is eligible

Employees with at least one year of service are eligible for paid parental leave. Employees with less than one year of service should talk with Human Resources about unpaid options.

Paid parental leave

A birth parent receives 12 weeks of paid leave. A non-birth parent, including an adoptive or foster parent, receives 6 weeks of paid leave. Leave must begin within six months of the birth or placement.

Giving notice

Tell Human Resources at least 30 calendar days before your leave begins, or as soon as you can if the timing is not predictable.

Caring for a family member

Employees with at least one year of service can take up to 4 weeks of paid leave a year to care for a seriously ill spouse, partner, child, or parent. A medical certification is required and goes only to Human Resources, never to your manager.
