Vacation and Time Off Policy

Version 6.1, current. Owner: Human Resources.

How much vacation you earn

Full-time employees earn 15 vacation days a year during their first five years of service and 20 vacation days a year after that. Part-time employees earn vacation in proportion to their scheduled hours.

Carrying vacation into next year

You can carry up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used within the first three months of that year or they are lost. Unused days above the 5-day limit are lost at the end of the year and are not paid out.

Requesting vacation

Requests of 5 business days or fewer need your manager's approval at least 3 business days ahead. Requests of more than 5 consecutive business days need your manager's approval at least 10 business days ahead.

When you don't have enough vacation days

You cannot take vacation days you have not earned, and your balance cannot go below zero. If a planned absence is longer than your balance, you can request up to 5 days of unpaid leave to cover the difference. Unpaid leave needs approval from both your manager and Human Resources.

Sick days

Full-time employees receive 8 paid sick days a year. Sick days don't carry over. A doctor's note is required for an absence of more than 3 consecutive business days.

Jury duty

Hartwell pays your regular wages for up to 10 business days of jury duty a year. Jury duty does not reduce your vacation or sick balance. Send a copy of your summons to Human Resources and tell your manager as soon as you receive it.

Bereavement leave

You receive up to 5 paid days off after the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, or sibling), and up to 2 paid days after the death of another relative. Tell your manager; no documentation is needed.

Hartwell Foods is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
