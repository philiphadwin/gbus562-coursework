Hartwell Foods Human Resources Intranet

Remote Work Policy

Version 2.2, current. Owner: Human Resources.

Who can work remotely

Office-based employees can work remotely after 90 days of employment. Warehouse, distribution, and production roles require on-site work and are not eligible.

How many days

Eligible employees can work remotely up to 2 days a week. Your manager chooses which days, based on your team's needs. More than 2 days a week needs written approval from your department director.

Working from another state

Working from another state for more than 10 business days in a year has tax consequences for Hartwell, so it needs approval from Human Resources first.

Equipment and expenses

Hartwell provides a laptop. It does not pay for home internet service, furniture, or utilities.

Hartwell Foods is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
