Hartwell Foods Human Resources Intranet

Reporting Workplace Concerns

Version 1.4, current. Owner: Human Resources Director.

What to report

Report harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety hazards, or anything else that makes you believe a person is being treated unfairly or is at risk.

How to report

Contact the Human Resources Director directly at hr.director@hartwellfoods.example. You can also go to any Human Resources team member. You don't need proof before you report, and you don't need to go to your manager first.

What happens next

A member of the Human Resources team, a person, reads every report and contacts you within 2 business days. Reports are shared only with the people who need to know in order to look into them.

No retaliation

Hartwell prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation.

Hartwell Foods is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
