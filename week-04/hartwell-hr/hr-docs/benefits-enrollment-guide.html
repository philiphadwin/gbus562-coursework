Benefits Enrollment Guide

Version 5.0, current. Owner: Human Resources.

When new employees enroll

New employees have 30 calendar days from their first day to choose health, dental, and vision coverage. If you don't enroll in time, you have no coverage until the next open enrollment.

Open enrollment

Open enrollment runs for two weeks each fall. Changes you make take effect at the start of the next calendar year. If you do nothing, your current choices continue, except for flexible spending accounts, which you must choose again each year.

Changing coverage after a life event

Marriage, divorce, a new child, or a spouse losing coverage are qualifying life events. You have 31 calendar days from the event to change your coverage. After that, you must wait for open enrollment.

Retirement plan

Employees can join the retirement plan after 60 days of employment. Hartwell matches contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of pay.
