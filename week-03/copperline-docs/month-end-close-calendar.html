Month-End Close Calendar

Current version. Owner: Finance Department, Controller's Office.

How the close calendar works

Every month, the Controller's Office closes the books so that the month's financial results are complete and accurate. The deadlines below are counted in business days. Business day 1 is the first business day of the new month. Business day minus 1 is the last business day of the month being closed.

Deadlines for all employees before month end

  • Business day minus 2: submit expense reports for any expenses from the month being closed, so they are recorded in that month.
  • Business day minus 1: managers approve all pending expense reports and purchase order receipts in their queue.

Accounts Payable cutoff

Vendor invoices received by noon on business day 1 are recorded in the month being closed. Invoices received after that are recorded in the new month. If you receive a vendor invoice directly, forward it to Accounts Payable the same day so it is not missed.

Accruals for goods and services not yet invoiced

If your department received goods or services during the month but has no invoice yet, send the Controller's Office an accrual request by business day 2. Include the vendor, a description, the estimated amount, and the purchase order number if there is one. Accruals over $10,000 need your department director's approval.

Controller's Office close tasks

  • Business day 3: account reconciliations completed for cash, receivables, payables, and inventory.
  • Business day 4: inventory valuation and cost of goods sold review completed.
  • Business day 5: preliminary results delivered to the Chief Financial Officer.
  • Business day 7: final close. After final close, the month is locked and no further entries are made.

Corrections after the month is locked

Errors found after final close are corrected in the current open month, not in the locked month. Corrections over $25,000 must be reported to the Chief Financial Officer with an explanation of the cause.
