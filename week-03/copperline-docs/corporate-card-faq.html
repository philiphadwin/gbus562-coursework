Corporate Card FAQ

Current version. Owner: Finance Department, Accounts Payable.

Who can get a corporate card?

Employees who travel for business at least four times a year, or who buy supplies for their department regularly, can request a Copperline corporate card. Your manager submits the request to Accounts Payable. Each card has a monthly limit of $5,000 and a single-purchase limit of $1,000. Your department director can request a higher limit.

Can I use my corporate card for personal purchases?

No. The corporate card is for business expenses only. If you charge a personal purchase by mistake, report it in the expense system and repay Copperline within 10 business days. Repeated personal use leads to the card being cancelled.

What should I do if my corporate card is lost or stolen?

Call the card issuer immediately using the 24-hour number on the back of your card's welcome letter or in the card app, so the card is frozen. Then tell Accounts Payable within one business day. You are not responsible for charges made after you report the card lost or stolen.

How do I reconcile my corporate card statement?

Each charge on your monthly statement must be matched to a business purpose in the expense system. Reconcile your statement within 5 business days after it closes. Attach a receipt for every charge over $25, the same receipt rule as the Travel and Expense Policy. Cards with unreconciled charges older than 60 days are suspended until the statement is complete.

Can I use my corporate card for a purchase that needs a purchase order?

No. Purchases over $1,000 need an approved purchase order under the Purchasing Approval Matrix and cannot be paid on a corporate card, even if your card limit would allow it.

What if a merchant declines my card?

Check the card app for a fraud alert and confirm the charge if it is yours. If the card is declined because of your limit, ask your manager whether the purchase should be split across months or handled by purchase order instead. Do not use a personal card and claim the cost later unless no other option is available.
