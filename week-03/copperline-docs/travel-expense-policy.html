Travel and Expense Policy

Version 4.0 (current). Replaces Version 3.2. Owner: Finance Department.

Purpose and who this policy covers

This Travel and Expense Policy explains which business expenses Copperline Manufacturing reimburses and how to claim them. It applies to every Copperline employee who travels or spends money on the company's behalf. When this policy and a manager's instruction disagree, this policy governs.

Meal limits by city tier

Copperline reimburses meals during business travel up to a per-person limit for each meal. The limit includes tax and tip. The limit depends on the city where the meal is eaten.

  • Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, Chicago, Washington DC, Los Angeles, and Seattle): breakfast up to $20, lunch up to $30, and dinner up to $75 per person.
  • Tier 2 cities (every other city in the United States): breakfast up to $15, lunch up to $25, and dinner up to $55 per person.

Any amount above the limit is the employee's own cost and is not reimbursed. Meals are reimbursed only on days when you are traveling overnight or attending an all-day business event away from your normal work location.

Receipts for meals and other expenses

A receipt is required for any single expense over $25, including meals. The receipt must show the vendor, the date, and the amount. An expense over $25 submitted without a receipt is not reimbursed unless your department director approves a written explanation.

Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable during ordinary business travel. The only exception is client entertainment approved in advance by your department director, as described below.

Client entertainment

Meals and events with customers or prospective customers are client entertainment, not travel meals, and the city-tier meal limits do not apply. Client entertainment must be approved in advance by your department director. The claim must name the client company and the people who attended, and state the business purpose.

Airfare

Book economy class through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Business class is reimbursable only for a single flight segment longer than 6 hours, with your vice president's approval. Seat upgrades and in-flight purchases are personal expenses.

Hotels

Book a standard room through the company travel portal. The nightly room rate may not exceed $250 in Tier 1 cities or $175 in Tier 2 cities, before taxes. Room service is reimbursed as a meal and counts toward that meal's limit.

Ground transportation

Rideshare services and licensed taxis are reimbursable for trips between your home or office and the airport, between the airport and your hotel, and between your hotel and business meetings. Choose the standard ride option. Premium, luxury, or extra-large ride options are not reimbursable unless no standard option is available. Tips on these rides are reimbursable up to 20 percent of the fare.

Rental cars are reimbursable only when they cost less than the rideshare or taxi trips they replace, or when no other transportation is practical. Choose a compact or midsize car and decline the rental company's insurance, because Copperline's corporate policy already covers rental vehicles.

Personal vehicle mileage

When you use your own vehicle for business, Copperline reimburses mileage at the standard federal mileage rate for business use. Your normal commute between home and your regular work location is not reimbursable.

Submitting an expense report

Submit your expense report in the expense system within 30 days after the trip ends or the expense is incurred. Attach every required receipt. Your manager approves reports under $2,500. Reports of $2,500 or more also need your department director's approval. Reports submitted more than 90 days after the expense are not reimbursed.
