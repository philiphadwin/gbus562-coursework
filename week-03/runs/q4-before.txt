Consumer Fee Schedule (Version 8) - Wire transfers

- Incoming domestic wire transfer: $10.
- Incoming international wire transfer: $15.
- Outgoing domestic wire transfer: $25.
- Outgoing international wire transfer: $45. The receiving bank or an intermediary bank may deduct its own fees from the amount sent.

Fee Refund Guide for Member Service Staff (Version 2) - Refunds that need a branch manager

A refund needs the branch manager's approval when the fee is more than $30, when the member has already had a refund in the last 90 days, or when the member asks for several fees to be refunded at once. A branch manager may approve refunds of up to $150 in total for one member in any 90-day period.

Fee Refund Guide for Member Service Staff (Version 2) - What a member service representative can approve

A member service representative may approve a refund of a single fee of $30 or less without anyone else's approval. A representative may approve only one refund for the same member in any 90-day period.

Fee Refund Guide for Member Service Staff (Version 2) - Refunds that need the Member Services Director

Refunds of more than $150 in total for one member in any 90-day period need the Member Services Director's approval. So does any refund for a business account.

ANSWER
The outgoing international wire transfer fee is $45 [consumer-fee-schedule.html]. A member service representative may only approve a refund of a single fee of $30 or less without anyone else's approval [fee-refund-guide.html]. Since $45 exceeds $30, this refund needs branch manager approval [fee-refund-guide.html].

Additional conditions noted in the sources: a refund also needs branch manager approval if the member has already had a refund in the last 90 days, or if the member asks for several fees to be refunded at once [fee-refund-guide.html]. If the total refunds for that member in a 90-day period exceed $150, or if it is for a business account, approval from the Member Services Director is required instead [fee-refund-guide.html].
