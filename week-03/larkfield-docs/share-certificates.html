Share Certificates

Version 5 (current). Owner: Deposit Products.

What a share certificate is

A share certificate is a savings account that pays a fixed dividend rate for a fixed term. In exchange for the higher rate, the member agrees to leave the money in the certificate until the term ends, which is called maturity. Share certificates are federally insured up to the legal limit, like other Larkfield savings accounts.

Terms and minimum deposit

Larkfield offers share certificates with terms of 6, 12, 24, 36, and 60 months. The minimum opening deposit for any share certificate is $500. A member can hold any number of share certificates.

Dividend rates

  • 6-month share certificate: 3.05% annual percentage yield (APY).
  • 12-month share certificate: 3.40% APY.
  • 24-month share certificate: 3.55% APY.
  • 36-month share certificate: 3.60% APY.
  • 60-month share certificate: 3.75% APY.

The rate is fixed when the certificate is opened and does not change during the term. Dividends are paid monthly and can stay in the certificate or be transferred to the member's savings or checking account.

Early withdrawal penalty

Taking money out of a share certificate before maturity costs a penalty. For terms of 12 months or less, the penalty is 90 days of dividends on the amount withdrawn. For terms longer than 12 months, the penalty is 180 days of dividends on the amount withdrawn. If the certificate has not yet earned enough dividends to cover the penalty, the rest is taken from the amount deposited. The penalty is waived if the owner dies or is declared legally incompetent.

At maturity

When a share certificate matures, it renews automatically for the same term at the rate in effect on the renewal day. The member has a 10-day grace period after maturity to withdraw the money or change the term with no penalty.

Adding money

Money cannot be added to a share certificate after it is opened. The one exception is the 12-month Add-On Certificate, which accepts additional deposits of $50 or more at any time during its term.
