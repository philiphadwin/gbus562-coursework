Consumer Fee Schedule

Version 8 (current). Replaces Version 7. Owner: Member Services.

About this fee schedule

This fee schedule lists the fees Larkfield Credit Union charges on consumer accounts: checking, savings, and the services attached to them. It applies to every member with a consumer account. Business accounts have a separate fee schedule.

Checking account monthly fees

Everyday Checking has no monthly service fee. Plus Checking has a monthly service fee of $8. The Plus Checking fee is waived for any month in which the account keeps a minimum daily balance of $1,000, or receives direct deposits totaling $500 or more.

Overdraft and returned item fees

When Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws a member's checking account, the overdraft fee is $29 per item paid. When Larkfield returns an item unpaid because the account does not have enough money, the returned item fee is $29 per item returned. No more than three overdraft or returned item fees are charged to one account in a single business day.

No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day.

Members can link a savings account for overdraft transfers. An overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free.

Stop payments

A stop payment on a check costs $30 per request. One request can cover a single check or a series of checks with consecutive numbers. A stop payment stays in effect for six months and can be renewed for another $30.

Wire transfers

  • Incoming domestic wire transfer: $10.
  • Incoming international wire transfer: $15.
  • Outgoing domestic wire transfer: $25.
  • Outgoing international wire transfer: $45. The receiving bank or an intermediary bank may deduct its own fees from the amount sent.

Official checks and money orders

A cashier's check costs $8. A money order costs $3. Members can buy up to five money orders in one day.

ATM and debit card fees

Withdrawals at Larkfield ATMs and at CO-OP network ATMs are free. A withdrawal at any other ATM costs $2, and the ATM's owner may charge its own fee as well. A replacement debit card costs $10, or $30 with rush delivery. A replacement card is free when the old card was reported lost or stolen because of fraud.

Statements and account research

Electronic statements are free. Paper statements cost $2 per month. Account research, such as finding an old transaction or reconciling an account, costs $20 per hour with a one-hour minimum. A copy of a paid check costs $3.

Dormant accounts

An account with no member-initiated activity for 24 months becomes dormant. A dormant account with a balance under $500 is charged $5 per month. Any deposit or withdrawal by the member ends dormant status.
