Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2. Superseded by Version 4.0. Owner: Finance Department.

Purpose and who this policy covers

This Travel and Expense Policy explains which business expenses Copperline Manufacturing reimburses and how to claim them. It applies to every Copperline employee who travels or spends money on the company's behalf.

Meal limits

Copperline reimburses meals during business travel up to a per-person limit for each meal, including tax and tip. The same limits apply in every city: breakfast up to $15, lunch up to $20, and dinner up to $50 per person. Any amount above the limit is the employee's own cost and is not reimbursed.

Receipts

A receipt is required for any single expense over $75. Expenses of $75 or less may be claimed without a receipt.

Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Airfare

Book economy class through the company travel portal. Business class is not reimbursable.

Hotels

Book a standard room through the company travel portal. The nightly room rate may not exceed $200 before taxes in any city.

Ground transportation

Licensed taxis are reimbursable for trips between the airport and your hotel. Rideshare services are not reimbursable. Rental cars require your manager's approval in advance.

Submitting an expense report

Submit your expense report within 60 days after the trip ends. Your manager approves all expense reports.
